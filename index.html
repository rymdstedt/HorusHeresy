Storylines

The Horus Heresy isn't one story but many, braided together. Each line below is a storyline you can follow. Add any of them to your route and they'll be woven into the reading order, every one of them converging on the Siege of Terra.

How to use this map

The Horus Heresy runs to 54 numbered books, followed by the ten-volume Siege of Terra and a shelf of novellas and prequels. Almost nobody needs all of them. This map shows every book as a station on coloured storyline lines, read left to right, and every line converges on Terra.

  1. Pick a route at the top: Express (the essentials), Main Campaign (every major storyline's key books) or Completionist.
  2. Add storylines you care about, such as the White Scars or Prospero. Their books get woven into your route.
  3. The glowing gold line on the map is your route. The numbers on the stations are your reading order.
  4. Open the Reading list to tick books off. Your progress is saved in this browser, and the link in your address bar shares your exact route.

How the routes were chosen

Reading order follows Black Library's recommended order, which is essentially publication order with the Siege of Terra novellas slotted in. The Express and Main Campaign tiers distil the advice that keeps coming up in community reading guides on Reddit (r/Horus_Heresy, r/40kLore, r/Warhammer40k), the Bolter & Chainsword forums and fan sites such as Track of Words:

  • Read the opening five books first: the trilogy plus Flight of the Eisenstein and Fulgrim.
  • Treat The First Heretic, Vengeful Spirit, The Master of Mankind, Praetorian of Dorn, Slaves to Darkness and The Buried Dagger as must-reads before the Siege.
  • The Sons of Horus and Word Bearers matter most at the start, while the Imperial Fists, Blood Angels and White Scars matter most at the end.
  • Follow the Legions you love, and skip the ones you don't.

The "read first" arrows and the key short stories come from Daunt's Horus Heresy Timeline on Kyle's Gaming, a story-arc driven chart built with the help of the Black Librarium guide. Select any book on the map to see arrows to the books you should read before it and the ones it leads into. Turn on Key short stories to add the gateway shorts and novellas, such as The Dark King → The Lightning Tower → The Crimson Fist → Templar → Praetorian of Dorn, to the map and to your route.

"Essential", "recommended" and "optional" are judgement calls, not gospel. Some fans would fight you over Know No Fear or Betrayer not being essential. If a storyline grabs you, follow it.

Spoilers

Book blurbs are kept spoiler-light: they describe each book's premise, not its outcome. Storyline descriptions mention which characters reach Terra, but never what happens there.

Not included

Most of the hundred-plus audio dramas and short stories. Only the key gateway shorts are listed individually; the rest are collected in the anthologies shown here. The post-Heresy follow-on series is also left out. For every short story, see Daunt's timeline linked above.
